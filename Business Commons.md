# Business Commons

**Shared Resources. Independent Enterprise.**

Business Commons is a modular, AI-assisted specification for helping businesses redesign, convert, and operate through shared infrastructure, cooperative arrangements, local marketplaces, independent enterprise, and resource-sharing models.

The specification enables an existing business to evaluate its current resources and generate alternative operating models, including cooperative marketplaces, shared workspaces, vendor rental arrangements, local supply networks, workforce exchanges, repair economies, mobile commerce, and shared business services.

Business Commons is designed to support businesses of different sizes and sectors without requiring a single ownership or operating model. A business can adopt only the modules relevant to its needs while independently adding sector-specific and optional plugin modules.

## Purpose

Business Commons provides an AI-assisted framework for transforming existing business infrastructure into shared economic infrastructure.

The specification can help businesses:

- Analyze existing facilities, assets, equipment, inventory, workforce, vendors, and operations.
- Identify underused or underutilized resources.
- Model alternative business structures.
- Evaluate cooperative and shared-resource arrangements.
- Create local marketplaces.
- Rent or share commercial space and equipment.
- Connect independent businesses with customers, workers, vendors, and suppliers.
- Coordinate local supply chains.
- Support repair, refurbishment, and remanufacturing.
- Facilitate mobile vendors and services.
- Coordinate shift work and shared labor pools.
- Assist with cooperative financing and shared insurance arrangements.
- Maintain transparency and product provenance.
- Generate transition plans from existing business models to alternative models.
- Provide AI-assisted recommendations while keeping major decisions under human control.

## Design Philosophy

Business Commons is based on the principle that businesses do not necessarily need to replace existing infrastructure to create new economic models.

Existing:

- Buildings
- Retail space
- Parking
- Warehouses
- Equipment
- Tools
- Distribution systems
- Technology
- Workforce
- Purchasing power
- Inventory
- Customer relationships

can become shared resources that enable independent businesses to participate in a larger economic network.

The specification does not require every implementation to become a cooperative. It supports cooperative, independent, hybrid, rental, revenue-sharing, shared-resource, and other business arrangements.
