# CommonsTruth

### Where Evidence Becomes Action.

CommonsTruth is an open-source public accountability platform that allows users to anonymously submit documented evidence of deceptive advertising, bait-and-switch tactics, false promotions, hidden fees, pricing discrepancies, and other misleading business practices.

The platform transforms isolated consumer experiences into structured public evidence that can reveal recurring patterns, support journalists and watchdog groups, and promote marketplace transparency.

---

## Core Mission

To provide the public with a privacy-focused, evidence-first platform where documented consumer reports can lead to awareness, accountability, reform, and action.

---

## Key Features

### Anonymous Reporting
- No account required for submissions
- Privacy-first reporting workflow
- Optional anonymous follow-up token for case updates
- VPN and privacy-browser friendly access

### Evidence Upload System
- Upload photos of receipts
- Upload advertisements and promotions
- Upload invoices or billing statements
- Upload screenshots of online offers
- Support for image, PDF, and document evidence
- Automatic file hashing for integrity verification

### Structured Incident Reports
- Business name
- Business location
- Date and time of incident
- Description of what occurred
- Product or service involved
- Amount advertised vs amount charged
- Employee/store response (optional)

### Fraud & Misconduct Categories
- Bait-and-switch
- False advertising
- Misleading discount claims
- Hidden fees
- Coupon refusal despite valid terms
- Shelf price vs register mismatch
- Subscription traps
- Undisclosed charges
- Fake limited-time offers
- Other deceptive business conduct

### Privacy Protections
- Metadata stripping from uploaded files
- EXIF removal from images
- Optional face/license plate blurring
- Hashed anti-spam identifiers
- No sale of user data
- Minimal data retention philosophy

### Verification & Trust Tools
- Duplicate report detection
- Similar incident clustering
- Multi-user corroboration system
- Confidence scoring model
- Moderator review tools
- Community flagging for abuse

### Public Transparency Dashboard
- Search by business name
- Search by city/state
- Category trend reports
- Incident timeline views
- Repeat-pattern detection
- Regional heat maps
- Most reported tactics dashboard

### Research & Journalism Tools
- Public API access
- Exportable datasets
- CSV / JSON reporting feeds
- Investigative trend summaries
- Consumer alert feeds

### Business Response Layer (Optional)
- Verified business response portal
- Correction / resolution updates
- Public rebuttal posting
- Dispute resolution records

### Moderation & Safety
- Abuse prevention systems
- Rate limiting
- Evidence authenticity review
- Defamation risk safeguards
- Neutral language enforcement
- Report appeal workflow

### Accessibility & UX
- Mobile-first reporting flow
- Fast upload experience
- Screen reader support
- Multi-language ready
- Clean public search interface

### Open Source Governance
- Fully open-source codebase
- Community contributions welcome
- AGPL-3.0+ licensed
- Transparent roadmap
- Public issue tracker
- Ethical governance model

## Example Use Cases

- A store advertises one price but charges another
- A coupon is denied despite valid terms
- Hidden mandatory fees appear at checkout
- Fake sale pricing compared to normal price
- Online offer differs from final billing
- Recurring deceptive promotions across locations

## Why CommonsTruth Matters

Consumers often experience deceptive practices individually and without recourse. CommonsTruth turns isolated incidents into documented patterns that can be analyzed, investigated, and acted upon.
