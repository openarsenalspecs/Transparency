# 🐝 Beekeeper's Guide to the Universe  
### Pollinator Ecology Intelligence System

An open-source, AI-assisted ecological platform for designing, simulating, and sustaining pollinator ecosystems. The system combines bee biology, habitat engineering, climate modeling, predator intelligence, visual diagnosis, and ecological simulation into a unified decision-support framework.

This project is licensed under **AGPL 3.0+** and is designed to remain open, extensible, and community-driven.

---

## 🌍 Core Vision

To create a living ecological intelligence system that helps users:

- Understand and support bee species in their natural and managed environments  
- Design pollinator-friendly landscapes using AI-assisted spatial planning  
- Identify threats, diseases, and environmental stressors through visual AI  
- Simulate ecological outcomes before making physical changes  
- Build sustainable habitats that support biodiversity over time  

---

# 🧠 System Architecture

The platform is built as a modular ecological intelligence system:

- Bee Biology Knowledge Layer  
- Habitat Engineering Layer  
- Climate & Microclimate Physics Layer  
- Plant & Succession Ecology Layer  
- Predator & Threat Intelligence Layer  
- Visual Diagnosis & Identification Layer  
- Ecological Simulation Lab Layer  
- Spatial Mapping & Visualization Layer  
- AI Reasoning & Knowledge Graph Core

# 🧩 Full Feature List

---

## 🐝 1. Bee Biology Knowledge System

- Species-specific modules (honey bees, bumble bees, mason bees, carpenter bees, native bees)  
- Lifecycle tracking and behavioral modeling  
- Seasonal care instructions  
- Forage preference databases  
- Climate adaptability profiles  
- Disease susceptibility mapping  

---

## 🧱 2. Habitat Engineering Module

- Hive construction guides (Langstroth, Warre, Top-bar systems)  
- Native bee habitat structures (tubes, blocks, ground nests, deadwood systems)  
- Climate-adaptive hive design logic  
- Maintenance scheduling systems  
- Habitat enticement engine for attracting native colonies  

---

## 🌦️ 3. Climate & Microclimate Simulation

- Sunlight exposure modeling  
- Wind flow and shelter analysis  
- Frost pocket and heat trap detection  
- Moisture and drainage prediction  
- Seasonal environmental stress mapping  
- Hive stability zone generation  

---

## 🌼 4. Plant & Succession Ecology Engine

- Regional plant and tree databases  
- Bloom cycle and seasonal forage planning  
- Nectar and pollen value scoring  
- Multi-year ecological succession modeling  
- Invasive species risk detection  
- Long-term canopy and habitat evolution simulation  

---

## 🪲 5. Predator & Threat Intelligence System

- Identification of bee predators (wasps, hornets, birds, mammals)  
- Parasite detection (Varroa mites, wax moths, etc.)  
- Robbing behavior detection  
- Environmental stress classification  
- Seasonal predator risk forecasting  
- Non-lethal ecological protection strategies  

---

## 🧫 6. Visual Diagnosis & Identification AI

- Image upload support for bees, predators, and hive conditions  
- Multi-hypothesis species identification  
- Disease and stress detection from visual cues  
- Confidence scoring system  
- Context-aware reasoning (season, climate, forage conditions)  
- Recommendation engine for next steps  

---

## 🗺️ 7. Spatial Mapping & 3D Visualization

- 2D land layout reconstruction from images  
- 3D terrain visualization support  
- Sunlight and wind overlay mapping  
- Hive placement optimization zones  
- Pollinator corridor design tools  
- Planting zone recommendation system  

---

## 🧪 8. Ecological Simulation Lab

- “What-if” ecosystem modeling  
- Hive placement simulation  
- Planting strategy comparison  
- Climate stress testing scenarios  
- Species interaction modeling  
- Long-term ecological forecasting  

---

## 🧠 9. AI Ecological Reasoning Core

- Cross-module ecological reasoning engine  
- Knowledge graph linking bees, plants, climate, predators, and disease  
- Explainable AI decision outputs  
- Uncertainty-aware recommendations  
- Conversational ecological assistant interface  

---

# 🔄 System Behavior Model

1. User uploads image or asks question  
2. Vision system identifies bees, predators, or environmental signals  
3. Climate and plant systems contextualize environment  
4. Habitat and predator systems evaluate risk and placement  
5. Simulation layer tests outcomes  
6. AI core synthesizes final recommendations  

---

# 🌿 Design Principles

- Ecological balance is prioritized over optimization  
- All AI outputs include confidence levels  
- No single-answer diagnoses when uncertainty exists  
- Non-destructive intervention is preferred  
- Long-term ecosystem health is modeled over short-term gains  
- Species-specific reasoning is required for all guidance
