# MIT Horizon

**Mapping MIT Innovation, Transparently.**

MIT Horizon is an open-source research intelligence platform designed to track MIT research projects, grants, patents, publications, startups, corporate collaborations, and public benefit outcomes. The goal is to provide a transparent, searchable, and community-driven system that shows how innovation flows from funding sources to real-world impact.

MIT Horizon highlights what becomes **open source**, what moves into **corporate holdings**, and what remains in hybrid or restricted forms. It is built for researchers, journalists, students, policymakers, developers, and the public.

---

## Core Mission

- Track MIT research and development activity across all departments and labs.
- Show funding sources and financial flows.
- Map research outputs such as publications, patents, software, and startups.
- Highlight ownership outcomes: public, open, private, or hybrid.
- Promote transparency, accountability, and public understanding of innovation systems.
