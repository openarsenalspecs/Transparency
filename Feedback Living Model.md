# Feedback Living Model (FLM)

**Understanding markets as evolving feedback organisms**

---

## Overview

Feedback Living Model (FLM) is an AGPL 3.0+ licensed systemic intelligence and simulation framework designed to model markets and industries as **living feedback systems**.

It treats the global economy as a continuously evolving organism composed of interconnected:

- capital flows  
- industries and supply chains  
- behavioral agents  
- regulatory constraints  
- narrative systems  
- structural dependencies  

Instead of predicting market outcomes, FLM simulates **how systemic pressures, feedback loops, and structural relationships generate pathways of expansion, instability, and collapse.**

---

## Core Concept

Markets are not static datasets.

They are:

> **evolving feedback organisms composed of interdependent economic, behavioral, and structural systems**

FLM models this by representing the economy as a **dynamic graph of recursive feedback loops**, where every node both influences and is influenced by the system.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Economic Ontology Core
A structured system that models:

- industries and sub-industries  
- companies and institutions  
- supply chains and dependencies  
- macroeconomic forces  
- financial instruments  
- regulatory constraints  

---

### 2. Capital Flow Engine
Tracks systemic liquidity and capital movement:

- institutional capital rotation  
- retail inflows and outflows  
- VC funding cycles  
- credit expansion and contraction  
- buybacks and reinvestment cycles  

---

### 3. Regime Detection System
Identifies macroeconomic phases:

- liquidity expansion  
- inflation shock regimes  
- credit tightening cycles  
- speculative mania phases  
- structural transition periods  
- contraction and recessionary states  

---

### 4. Signal Detection Engine
Detects early systemic instability patterns:

- valuation vs earnings divergence  
- leverage acceleration  
- margin compression  
- hiring decoupling from revenue  
- inventory accumulation  
- volatility regime compression  

---

### 5. Fragility Index System
Quantifies systemic vulnerability:

- dependency concentration  
- leverage exposure  
- revenue stability  
- supply chain sensitivity  
- contagion potential  

Outputs a **systemic fragility map** across sectors and entities.

---

### 6. Shock Library
A structured catalog of stress events:

- interest rate shocks  
- liquidity freezes  
- commodity price spikes  
- regulatory interventions  
- demand collapses  
- supply chain disruptions  

Used for controlled stress testing.

---

### 7. Cascade Simulation Engine
Models propagation of shocks through the system:

- dependency chain breakdowns  
- financial contagion pathways  
- secondary and tertiary failures  
- systemic amplification effects  

Produces **collapse and instability pathways**.

---

### 8. Feedback Loop Modeler
Simulates reflexive economic behavior:

- layoffs → demand reduction → further layoffs  
- credit tightening → investment contraction  
- speculation → valuation expansion → correction  

Models markets as **self-reinforcing dynamic systems**.

---

### 9. Capital Flow Tracking Layer
Analyzes liquidity structure:

- inflow/outflow imbalances  
- capital exhaustion points  
- sector rotation cycles  
- liquidity concentration zones  

---

### 10. Counterfactual Engine
Runs alternative scenario simulations:

- no interest rate hikes  
- sustained liquidity environment  
- altered supply chain conditions  
- removed demand shocks  

Helps isolate structural vs cyclical drivers.

---

### 11. Multi-Timeframe System
Analyzes economic dynamics across:

- short-term (weeks)  
- mid-term (months)  
- long-term (years)  

---

### 12. Narrative Intelligence Layer
Tracks belief systems driving markets:

- dominant economic narratives  
- narrative strength vs fundamentals  
- hype saturation levels  
- divergence between perception and reality  

---

### 13. Agent-Based Simulation Engine
Simulates adaptive behavior of:

- consumers  
- corporations  
- financial institutions  
- governments  
- investors  

Each agent responds dynamically to system conditions.

---

### 14. Early Warning System
Detects convergence of systemic risk signals:

- liquidity tightening + leverage buildup  
- narrative peak + valuation divergence  
- fragility spike + capital reversal  

Outputs pre-instability alerts.

---

### 15. Scenario Generator (LLM Layer)
Generates structured systemic scenarios:

- trigger events  
- cascade pathways  
- affected sectors  
- amplification nodes  
- alternative outcomes  
- confidence ranges  

---

### 16. Explanation & Audit Layer
Ensures interpretability:

- reasoning traces  
- assumptions  
- uncertainty ranges  
- alternative explanations  
- failure conditions  

---

## System Philosophy

FLM does not attempt to predict markets.

It models:

> how systems behave when pushed through cycles of expansion, stress, and collapse

Markets are treated as:

> recursive feedback organisms that evolve through interaction, constraint, and belief.
