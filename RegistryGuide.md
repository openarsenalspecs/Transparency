# RegistryGuide

**Building Trust Into Every Title Transfer.**

RegistryGuide is an open-source vehicle registration, title management, and legal compliance platform designed for government agencies, departments of motor vehicles, tribal governments, territories, and transportation authorities. Built with a modular architecture, RegistryGuide enables jurisdictions to manage registrations, titles, ownership records, inspections, taxes, legal requirements, and vehicle transactions through a unified system.

The platform combines a modern web interface, machine-readable law modules, AI-powered assistance, and optional blockchain-backed title verification to create a secure, transparent, and scalable vehicle administration ecosystem.

## Federated State Architecture

RegistryGuide is designed as a federated platform. Each state, territory, tribal government, or jurisdiction can maintain its own database, legal rules, forms, fees, workflows, and optional blockchain node while sharing a common open-source codebase.

This approach allows jurisdictions to maintain regulatory independence while enabling secure interoperability for interstate vehicle transactions and title verification.
