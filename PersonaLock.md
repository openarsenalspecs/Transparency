# PersonaLock

**Editing is the product, not generation.**

PersonaLock is an open source, modular, identity-first AI platform designed to preserve a person's authentic voice, reasoning, values, evidence standards, and editorial preferences. Rather than acting solely as a writing assistant, PersonaLock serves as an identity governance layer that evaluates, validates, and refines AI-generated content to ensure it remains faithful to its owner.

Built around a native identity-aware language model, PersonaLock combines long-term memory, a searchable publication archive, research capabilities, writing assistance, and explainable governance into a unified platform. Every decision is traceable, every profile change requires explicit approval, and every published output is evaluated for consistency before it reaches the user.

**Core Philosophy**

> Editing is the product, not generation.

Generation is inexpensive.

Identity is irreplaceable.
