# EdgeLens

EdgeLens is an open specification for **privacy-preserving edge computer vision systems** designed to scale from localhost deployments to enterprise and institutional networks. It enables organizations such as schools, universities, and enterprises to process sensitive visual data locally while still participating in community-driven model improvement through federated and privacy-preserving learning systems.

EdgeLens is built to ensure that **no sensitive visual data ever leaves trusted environments by default**, while still enabling global collaboration, model improvement, and verifiable AI pipelines.

---

## Core Philosophy

- Privacy is the default, not an option
- Data stays local, insights travel
- Open collaboration without raw data exposure
- Edge-first, cloud-optional architecture
- Modular, composable system design
- Verifiable and auditable AI pipelines
- Institution-controlled governance

## Long-Term Vision

EdgeLens aims to become a foundational open standard for **privacy-preserving computer vision systems**, enabling institutions to safely adopt advanced AI vision capabilities without surrendering control of sensitive data.

By combining edge-first inference, federated learning, verifiable provenance, and modular governance, EdgeLens allows organizations to collaborate on improving AI models while ensuring that sensitive visual data remains fully protected within trusted environments.
