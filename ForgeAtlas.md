# ForgeAtlas

**Lifecycle-Aware Engineering at Scale**

ForgeAtlas is an open-source AGPL 3.0+ intelligence system for designing, sourcing, and optimizing physical products with a focus on durability, repairability, and real-world material constraints. It combines engineering intelligence, local-first material sourcing, and real-time market pricing into a unified lifecycle optimization platform.

The goal of ForgeAtlas is simple: **end planned obsolescence by defaulting engineering decisions toward longevity and real-world viability.**

---

## Core Vision

ForgeAtlas treats physical product design as a living system constrained by:

- Material science realities  
- Regional supply availability  
- Market price volatility  
- Repairability and lifecycle cost  
- Environmental and structural durability  

Instead of designing in isolation, ForgeAtlas continuously asks:

> “Can this be built locally, affordably, and made to last?”

---

## Key Features

### 1. Durable Design Optimization Engine
- Detects weak points and failure-prone structures in designs  
- Identifies planned obsolescence patterns (glued assemblies, sealed systems, proprietary parts)  
- Suggests structural redesigns to increase lifespan  
- Recommends modular, repairable architectures  
- Outputs durability improvement scoring per revision  

---

### 2. Failure Prediction & Stress Analysis
- Maps mechanical stress concentrations  
- Predicts likely failure timelines  
- Evaluates thermal, chemical, and fatigue degradation risks  
- Identifies single-point-of-failure components  
- Suggests reliability improvements at design stage  

---

### 3. Material Substitution Intelligence
- Recommends stronger or longer-lasting alternative materials  
- Matches equivalents based on strength, corrosion resistance, and fatigue tolerance  
- Converts proprietary materials into standardized alternatives  
- Optimizes for durability, cost, and availability balance  
- Suggests repair-friendly fastening systems  

---

### 4. Local-First Material Sourcing Engine
- Prioritizes nearby suppliers and distributors  
- Integrates salvage, reclaimed, and surplus material markets  
- Maps regional material availability within configurable radius  
- Provides distance, logistics cost, and carbon impact estimates  
- Ensures designs remain buildable using local constraints  

---

### 5. Supplier & Material Intelligence Graph
- Builds dynamic graph of suppliers and materials  
- Tracks pricing, availability, and reliability across vendors  
- Connects materials to multiple sourcing channels  
- Enables cross-supplier comparison and ranking  
- Maintains regional supply ecosystem awareness  

---

### 6. Real-Time Material Market Intelligence
- Aggregates live pricing from suppliers and marketplaces  
- Tracks price volatility and market trends  
- Detects shortages and demand spikes  
- Provides buy-now vs wait recommendations  
- Identifies seasonal material cycles (wood, steel, resin, etc.)  

---

### 7. Price Normalization & Fair Value Engine
- Converts raw material prices into lifecycle value metrics  
- Normalizes cost per strength, durability, and lifespan  
- Detects inflated pricing and regional markup anomalies  
- Provides fair market price estimation per region  
- Enables cost-per-performance comparisons  

---

### 8. Design-to-Supply Constraint Optimization
- Adjusts designs based on actual material availability  
- Recalculates structural integrity when substitutions occur  
- Maintains build feasibility under supply constraints  
- Rebalances load distribution for alternative materials  
- Continuously updates BOM based on real-time conditions  

---

### 9. Lifecycle Simulation System
- Estimates product lifespan under different configurations  
- Models maintenance cycles and repair frequency  
- Calculates long-term ownership cost  
- Compares environmental impact across designs  
- Simulates durability vs cost tradeoffs  

---

### 10. Circular Economy & Salvage Integration
- Prioritizes reclaimed and recycled materials  
- Integrates demolition and industrial reuse streams  
- Treats salvage as first-class engineering input  
- Reduces reliance on virgin material production  
- Supports closed-loop manufacturing systems  

---

### 11. Optimized Bill of Materials (BOM) Generator
- Generates multi-tier BOMs (primary, fallback, salvage)  
- Includes cost, durability, and availability metrics  
- Updates dynamically based on market changes  
- Exports in CSV, JSON, and engineering formats  
- Aligns BOM with regional supply constraints  

---

### 12. Procurement & Build Planning System
- Step-by-step sourcing instructions  
- Optimized supplier selection and ordering strategy  
- Timing recommendations based on market cycles  
- Batch purchasing optimization  
- Build-ready procurement roadmap  

---

### 13. Repairability & Modularity Scoring
- Scores designs based on ease of repair  
- Encourages standardized fasteners and modular components  
- Penalizes sealed or non-serviceable systems  
- Promotes upgradeable design pathways  
- Provides repair accessibility index  

---

### 14. Engineering Report Generator
- Produces full lifecycle-aware engineering documentation  
- Includes redesign justification and material logic  
- Outputs manufacturer-ready specifications  
- Provides alternative design iterations  
- Human-readable and machine-structured formats  

---

### 15. Open AGPL Architecture
- Fully AGPL 3.0+ licensed system  
- Modular and extensible service-based design  
- Plugin-ready material and supplier integrations  
- Transparent evaluation and scoring logic  
- Designed for reproducibility and open collaboration  

---

## System Philosophy

ForgeAtlas is built on a single principle:

> **If a product cannot be repaired, sourced locally, or built to last—it is a design failure, not a consumer expectation.**
